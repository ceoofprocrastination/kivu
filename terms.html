1. Using KivU

KivU is an independent journal that runs in your browser. There is no account to create. You must be at least 13 years old, or 16 where local law sets a higher digital age of consent (see the Privacy Policy). KivU is still in development, so features may change or be removed.

2. Your writing and our work

You keep every right in what you write. We claim none of it, and because entries are encrypted on your device, we can’t access them anyway. KivU’s name, branding, design and original source code are protected. The repository is currently marked UNLICENSED: you may use the app, but you may not copy, redistribute or reuse the code or branding without permission. Third-party components stay under their own licences (see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

3. Your PIN and your backups

Your journal is encrypted with a key derived from your PIN, and the PIN is never stored. If you forget it, nobody can recover your entries, including us. Clearing browser data, losing a device, uninstalling a browser or switching browser profiles can permanently erase local entries. Export a backup regularly. Exports are plain JSON files, so keep them somewhere private.

4. Not medical or crisis care

Mood check-ins are personal reflections, not diagnoses or treatment. If you might be in danger or are thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country right away.

5. Acceptable use
