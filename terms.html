02. What Kivu is

Kivu is a personal journaling application with a writing space and self-reported mood check-ins. Features may change while the project is in development. Kivu is not a medical service, mental-health treatment, emergency service, or substitute for professional advice.

03. Your writing and privacy

The current app is designed to store journal entries in a local database on the device or browser profile where you use it. This project does not currently provide a Kivu account system or first-party cloud sync. Local storage is not the same as encrypted storage; people with access to your device or profile may be able to access your entries. Use device security appropriate to your circumstances.

The desktop edition is not available yet. Kivu does not upload journal entries to GitHub or to a first-party cloud service.

04. Use of the app

Kivu does not use AI models. Mood history reflects only the check-ins you choose to record and is not a medical assessment.

05. Backups and exports

You are responsible for keeping backups of important entries. Browser and desktop profiles may be separate. Export a backup before uninstalling, clearing browser data, changing devices, or testing a new release. Importing a backup can change existing records; review the app's import prompts and keep an original copy.
