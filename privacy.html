The plain-language version

Your journal is local.
Your rights are not.

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Version: 1.0

The short version. KivU is a local-first journal. Journal entries and mood check-ins are encrypted on your device with a key derived from your PIN. There is no KivU account and no first-party cloud sync. We do not sell or share personal information, we do not use AI models on your entries, and we do not run advertising or behavioural analytics. The main privacy risk is your own device and your own backups.

1. Who is responsible

KivU is an independent software project. For privacy purposes, the controller is the project maintainer. Contact: privacy@kivu.app (configure and monitor this mailbox before public launch). Postal address and, where required, an EU representative under Article 27 GDPR will be published in the imprint before commercial distribution in the EU/EEA.

2. What KivU is

KivU is a personal journaling application with a writing space, self-reported mood check-ins, local export/import, and an optional installable web app. It is not a medical device, a mental-health service, a crisis service, or a substitute for professional advice.

3. Data we process

3.1 Journal content you create

Entry titles, entry text, mood check-ins, timestamps, and optional generated mood-summary images. Journal entries and check-ins are stored in IndexedDB on your device and encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your PIN with PBKDF2-SHA-256. The key is not stored. We cannot decrypt your journal, and we do not receive it.

Mood check-ins can reveal information about your mental or physical health. Under GDPR this may be special category data (Article 9). We process it only because you deliberately enter it and create a PIN, which is your explicit consent to local processing for the journal’s core purpose.

3.2 Local settings

Theme preference, onboarding status, the vault salt/metadata, and the legal-acceptance timestamp are stored in your browser’s localStorage. These are strictly necessary for the app to function and are not used for tracking.

3.3 Technical data from the website

If you visit the public marketing pages, the hosting provider may process standard server-log data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamp for security and availability. We do not combine this with journal content. If you use the journal app, third-party CDN requests described in section 6 may disclose your IP address and user agent to those CDNs.

3.4 What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, phone number, or password.
  • No advertising identifiers, cross-site tracking, or behavioural analytics.
  • No precise geolocation, camera, microphone, contacts, or health-platform data.
  • No AI training data and no AI-generated analysis of your entries.

4. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Contract / requested service — Article 6(1)(b): providing the journal, local encryption, export/import, and settings.
  • Explicit consent — Article 9(2)(a): processing mood check-ins and journal content that may reveal health information. You consent by choosing to enter it; you can withdraw by deleting the data.
  • Legitimate interests — Article 6(1)(f): keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and maintaining availability. We balance these against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — Article 6(1)(c): responding to valid legal requests and maintaining required records.

5. Local storage and cookies

KivU does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It uses localStorage for strictly necessary preferences and vault metadata, and IndexedDB for encrypted journal content. These are functional, not tracking technologies. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the browser can delete your journal; export a backup first.

6. Third-party requests

The web app currently loads Tailwind CSS and Dexie from public CDNs. Those requests disclose your IP address and user agent to the CDN provider and are necessary for the app’s styling and local database layer. We do not send entry content to those CDNs. Google Fonts is not used; the app uses system fonts. If you require a zero-third-party build, self-host Tailwind and Dexie before deployment.

Hosting providers and CDNs may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an applicable adequacy decision. Journal content is never sent to them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability. Because your journal is local, you exercise most of these directly in the app:

  • Access / portability: use “Export backup” in the You tab.
  • Erasure: delete individual entries or use “Erase all data”.
  • Rectification: edit any entry.
  • Withdraw consent: delete the relevant check-ins or erase all data.
  • Object / restrict: stop using the app and clear local storage.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. We will respond to verified requests within the time required by law. Contact privacy@kivu.app.

8. California notice at collection (CCPA/CPRA)

We collect the categories of information described in section 3 for the purposes described there. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than providing the service. You have the right to know, delete, correct, and port your information, and to non-discrimination. Because the journal is local, most requests are fulfilled through the in-app export and erase controls. Authorised agents may submit requests with written permission. Contact privacy@kivu.app.

9. Children and age

KivU is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires a higher digital age of consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The vault setup requires you to confirm you meet the minimum age. If you believe a child has used the app, delete the local data and contact us.

10. Security

Journal content is encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM and a PBKDF2-derived key. The PIN is not stored. The app locks after inactivity, clears decrypted fields from the interface on lock, and does not transmit entries to a first-party server. No system is perfectly secure: someone with access to your unlocked device, your PIN, or an unencrypted export can read your journal. Choose a long PIN, keep your device updated, and store backups privately.

11. Retention

Journal content remains on your device until you delete it or clear browser storage. We do not hold a server copy. Export files remain wherever you save them. Hosting logs are retained only as long as necessary for security and legal obligations.

12. International transfers

We do not transfer journal content internationally because we do not receive it. Website hosting and CDN providers may process technical request data in other countries under appropriate safeguards.

13. Breach notification

Because journal content is local, a breach of our servers would not expose it. If we become aware of a breach affecting personal data we control, we will notify affected users and authorities as required. If your device or PIN is compromised, erase local data, change any reused credentials, and notify us so we can advise.

14. No AI, no sale, no advertising

KivU does not use AI models, machine-learning analysis, automated decision-making, or profiling on your journal. We do not sell personal information, share it for behavioural advertising, or use it as a marketing asset.

15. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app develops. Material changes will be noted by the effective date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.
