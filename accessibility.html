Conformance status: partially conforms with WCAG 2.2 Level AA

KivU aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA and the accessibility requirements of the European Accessibility Act and the ADA. A formal third-party audit has not yet been completed, so we describe the current status honestly rather than claiming full conformance.

Measures we have taken

  • Semantic landmarks, a skip link, logical heading order, and visible focus indicators with at least 3:1 contrast.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, dialogs, mood buttons, and editor controls.
  • Labels for the title, journal textarea, PIN fields, and icon-only buttons.
  • inert applied to background content while the vault, onboarding, or history dialog is open.
  • Focus moved into dialogs and returned to the trigger when they close; Escape closes the history sheet.
  • No colour-only state indicators: active tabs use aria-current; selected moods use aria-pressed plus a border.
  • Support for prefers-reduced-motion, prefers-contrast, and forced-colors.
  • Text can be zoomed to 200% without loss of function; the chart scrolls horizontally on small screens.
  • The mood chart has a visually hidden table alternative for screen-reader users.
  • Accessible authentication: PIN fields allow paste and declare the correct autocomplete values so password managers can assist.
  • Status messages use live regions; toasts pause on hover or focus and include a dismiss button.

Known limitations

  • Third-party CDN scripts — Tailwind CSS and Dexie are loaded from public CDNs. Their accessibility is outside our control. A self-hosted build is planned.
  • Canvas share image — the generated weekly image is decorative and optional; it is not the only way to access mood history. A text summary is available in the Mood tab.
  • PIN recovery — the PIN cannot be recovered by design. This is a security trade-off, not an accessibility feature.
  • Electron desktop build — not yet publicly distributed. The web app is the supported version.

Feedback and enforcement

If you meet a barrier, contact accessibility@kivu.app or open an issue at github.com/ceoofprocrastination/kivu/issues. Tell us the page, the assistive technology you use, and what happened. We aim to acknowledge accessibility reports within 5 working days.

EU/EEA consumers may also contact the national market-surveillance authority responsible for the European Accessibility Act in their country. US users may contact the relevant federal agency or file an ADA complaint.
