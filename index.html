THE KIVU PRINCIPLE / 002

Keep the mess.
That's the music.

We made Kivu for the part of your life that doesn't fit neatly into a caption. The thought you keep circling. The tiny detail that somehow stayed with you. The day that was ordinary until one strange, lovely thing happened.

There's no score for being a person here. No streak to defend. No pressure to turn pain into progress. Write when you want, leave when you need, come back when it feels right.

BUILT TO BE YOURS / 003

Your inner life isn't a growth metric.

The journal is designed for local-first writing without requiring an account. Your writing stays in this browser profile, so keep a backup before changing devices or clearing browser data. Kivu uses no AI models, and your private entries are never used as a marketing asset.

THE PRACTICAL STUFF / 004

Good to
know.

Straight answers, no theatre.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the current local-first journal. Your writing stays in this browser profile, so export a backup before changing devices or clearing browser data.

Does Kivu send my writing to a server?

The core journal uses a local database and this project does not include first-party cloud sync. Kivu does not use AI models. Your writing is stored locally in your browser profile.

Is there a desktop app?

Not yet. The desktop edition is coming later. For now, use Kivu in your browser and add it to your home screen or install it from your browser menu when supported.

What if I don't write for weeks?

Nothing happens. No streak breaks, no guilt, no message chasing you. Your page will be here when you want it.
